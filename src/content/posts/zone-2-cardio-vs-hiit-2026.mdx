## Use a Five-Part Scorecard

Score each option on fit, risk, cost, convenience, and exit path. Fit prevents returns and hidden work. Risk tells you what can fail and who fixes it. ## FAQ

**Is the premium option always safer?**  
No. Premium can buy support and materials, but only when those advantages match your use case. Verify compatibility and maintenance first.

**How often should I revisit the decision?**  
Review annually or whenever your household, account, vehicle, device ecosystem, or health routine changes.

**What should I document?**  
Keep receipts, model numbers, setup notes, warranty terms, and any settings that would be hard to recreate.

**What is the simplest rule of thumb?**  
Buy the most boring option that reliably solves the core job with the least future maintenance.
